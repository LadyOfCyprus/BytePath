Product Design Intern

Northstar Digital Labs · Makati City · Hybrid

About the internship

Work with our product team to research user needs and design clear, accessible interfaces for web-based services.

Learn through real product work, thoughtful feedback, and close collaboration with designers and developers. Bring your curiosity and a willingness to explore.

What you'll work on

  • Create wireframes and high-fidelity interfaces.
  • Support usability testing and summarize findings.
  • Maintain reusable Figma patterns and design system components.
  • Collaborate with developers on handoff specifications.

What you'll bring

  • Enrolled in IT, Computer Science, Multimedia Arts, or a related undergraduate program.
  • Familiar with Figma and user-centered design principles.
  • Able to explain design decisions clearly.
  • Available for the four-month internship, June–September 2027.

Before you apply

A few things to have ready. You can save your application as a draft anytime.

REQUIRED:
  • PDF résumé · up to 5 MB
  • Your availability window
  • A short statement of interest
Portfolio recommended, not required

Class projects, personal work, and prototypes are welcome. Show us how you think.
